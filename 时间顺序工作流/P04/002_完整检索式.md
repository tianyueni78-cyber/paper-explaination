# 完整检索式

## 结论

采用六组互补检索式，先覆盖问题域，再分别追踪区域、邻域、预算、RL超启发式和原方法引文链。数据库只做字段语法改写，不改变概念组合。

```text
S1: ("flexible job shop" OR FJSP OR "job shop") AND
    (AGV OR "automated guided vehicle" OR "limited transportation resource") AND
    (multi-objective OR NSGA-II OR evolutionary)

S2: (scheduling OR "flexible job shop") AND
    ("critical path" OR bottleneck OR region OR "regional partition" OR top-k) AND
    ("local search" OR neighborhood OR neighbourhood)

S3: (scheduling OR metaheuristic) AND
    ("adaptive neighborhood" OR "adaptive operator selection" OR "operator management") AND
    (reinforcement learning OR Q-learning OR bandit)

S4: ("local search" OR hyper-heuristic OR metaheuristic) AND
    (budget OR "computational budget" OR "evaluation budget" OR "resource allocation")

S5: (scheduling OR optimization) AND
    ("reinforcement learning hyper-heuristic" OR
     "learning hyper-heuristic" OR "Q-learning" AND operator)

S6: DOI("10.1016/j.swevo.2024.101658") OR
    exact-title("A Q-Learning based NSGA-II for dynamic flexible job shop scheduling with limited transportation resources")
```

时间范围原则：奠基性研究不设下限；“最新直接近邻”重点覆盖2023—2026；语言为中文或英文；文献类型含正式论文、会议论文和可稳定访问的预印本。
